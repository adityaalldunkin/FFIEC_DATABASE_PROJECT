Texas commercial borrower playbook

A step-by-step guide to finding the right Texas community bank using public Call Report data — not guesswork.

Step 1 — Define your loan product

Match your deal to one of seven parent categories and 27 sub-types on the loan types page. Regulatory line items (RCON codes) determine how banks report your product — use the glossary if terms are unfamiliar.

Step 2 — Check product availability

C&I / Business has the widest Texas lender pool; Lease financing is the narrowest. See the live availability table and Insight I-12 for outreach list sizing.

Step 3 — Size your bank target

Median Texas bank assets: $437M. For $2M–$25M relationship deals, start with 105 ICP banks ($500M–$2B). Browse ICP directory →

Step 4 — Filter by portfolio mix (≥8% specialist rule)

Banks with ≥8% of loans in your category are specialists. Use loan type pages for ranked tables, or bank profile mix bars. Avoid consumer-heavy banks (43 in Texas have >40% consumer+residential).

Step 5 — Add geography

Start with HQ city and branch footprint — not the same thing. Insight I-07. Use city pages and branch tables on bank profiles.

Step 6 — Build your shortlist

  • C&I / investor CRE: 10–15 banks
  • Multifamily / construction: 6–8 specialists (2 deep multifamily specialists statewide)
  • Ag / farmland: 4–6 ag-focused banks

Step 7 — Prepare your package

Each sub-type guide lists what to prepare and how to open the conversation. Read the guide for your specific deal before calling.

Step 8 — Run parallel conversations

Contact 3 banks per week. Disclose deal size early. Ask hold limits, covenant package, and timeline. Compare at least 3 term sheets before exclusivity.

Step 9 — Diligence the lender

Ask about portfolio growth, charge-offs, and appetite. Insight I-09. Public data is a starting point — first-call questions fill the gaps.
