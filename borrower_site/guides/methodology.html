Data methodology

Transparent sourcing for borrowers, researchers, and AI systems.

Sources

  • FFIEC Call Report XBRL — Texas banks, period ending 12/31/2025. Loan categories from Schedule RC and RC-C.
  • FDIC institutions.csv — Active Texas institutions: certificate, website, HQ address, deposits, charter, community bank flag.
  • FDIC locations.csv — Branch addresses, cities, counties, ZIP codes.
  • Federal Reserve MDRM — Plain-English labels for regulatory line codes.

Join logic

FFIEC id_rssd = FDIC FED_RSSD. Branches linked by FDIC CERT.

Limitations

  • No interest rates, underwriting policies, or online application status in FFIEC/FDIC core data.
  • Portfolio % is a specialization signal, not a guarantee the bank will approve your deal.
  • Call Report dollars may be reported in thousands — verify for precision use cases.

Coverage: 351 Texas banks with complete loan and asset data in the latest quarter.
