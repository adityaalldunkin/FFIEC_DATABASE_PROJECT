Agricultural operating line in Texas

Seasonal credit for seed, fertilizer, livestock feed, and harvest expenses.

Who this is for

Producers financing annual production cycles with repayment after harvest or sale.

How community banks underwrite it

Revolving lines tied to crop/livestock plan, insurance, and marketing. FSA guarantees may be available.

Parent category: Agricultural & Farmland · FFIEC: RCON1420, RCON1590 — farmland and agricultural production

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Crop/livestock plan
  • Crop insurance
  • Historical production records
  • Marketing contracts if any
  • Equipment and land schedule

How to approach a bank

Opening: Our [operation type] needs a [$] operating line for [crop season/livestock cycle].

Questions to ask:

  • Advance timing vs planting?
  • Crop insurance requirements?
  • FSA guarantee eligibility?

Operating vs equipment?
