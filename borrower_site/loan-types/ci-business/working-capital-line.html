Working capital line of credit in Texas

Revolving credit for inventory, payroll, and seasonal cash needs.

Who this is for

Operating businesses with fluctuating cash conversion cycles — distributors, manufacturers, services.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks set borrowing base on receivables and inventory, review quarterly financials, and require personal guarantees from owners.

Parent category: Commercial & Industrial (C&I) · FFIEC: RCON1766 — commercial and industrial loans

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • AR/AP aging
  • Inventory listing
  • 3 years financials + YTD P&L
  • Debt schedule
  • Personal financial statements

How to approach a bank

Opening: Our [business] needs a [$] revolver to support [seasonal growth/inventory]. Revenues are [$] with [margin] EBITDA.

Questions to ask:

  • Borrowing base formula?
  • Covenant package?
  • Annual cleanup requirement?
