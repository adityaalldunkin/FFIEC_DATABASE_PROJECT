Commercial Construction in Texas

Ground-up construction and major renovation for commercial and multifamily projects.

Understanding this loan type

Construction loans fund development draws and convert to permanent financing at completion. Mapped from RCONF158 (1–4 family construction) and RCONF159 (other construction & land development).

FFIEC line items: RCONF158, RCONF159 — construction and land development
