Land development & lot loans in Texas

Finance horizontal development — roads, utilities, plats — for commercial or residential lots.

Who this is for

Developers preparing entitled land for sale to builders or vertical developers.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks look at absorption, lot takedown pace, and infrastructure completion. Shorter tenors until lots sell.

Parent category: Commercial Construction · FFIEC: RCONF158, RCONF159 — construction and land development

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Plat and entitlement docs
  • Development budget by phase
  • Market study for lot absorption
  • Environmental and geotech
  • Offtake or builder interest letters

How to approach a bank

Opening: We're developing [lot count] lots in [city/subdivision] with phase 1 infrastructure cost of [$].

Questions to ask:

  • Advance rate on raw vs improved lots?
  • Do you require presales?
  • Interest reserve and maturity?

FAQ

Raw land vs improved lots?

Raw land is higher risk; improved platted lots are easier to finance.

Not this product: Not long-term hold of unentitled land without development plan.
