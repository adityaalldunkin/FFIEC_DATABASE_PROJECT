Investor CRE (income property) in Texas

Office, retail, industrial, and mixed-use held as investments and leased to tenants.

Understanding this loan type

Investor CRE is repaid primarily from tenant rent. Mapped from RCONF161 (other nonfarm nonresidential) and related commercial real estate lines. This is often the largest commercial bucket for Texas regionals.

FFIEC line items: RCONF161, RCONF162 — income-producing commercial real estate

FAQ

What is investor CRE?

Commercial property you lease to tenants — office, retail, industrial, not your own business.

CRE vs multifamily?

Multifamily is a CRE subtype; investor CRE here includes office, retail, and industrial income property.
