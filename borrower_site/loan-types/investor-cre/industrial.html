Investor industrial / warehouse loan in Texas

Financing for warehouses, distribution, and light industrial leased to operators.

Who this is for

Investors in bulk warehouse, flex, or last-mile distribution assets along Texas logistics corridors.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks favor clear height, dock count, truck courts, and creditworthy logistics tenants. E-commerce and 3PL demand supports many Texas deals.

Parent category: Investor CRE (income property) · FFIEC: RCONF161, RCONF162 — income-producing commercial real estate

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Rent roll and lease terms
  • Building specs (clear height, docks, power)
  • Tenant industry and credit
  • Environmental report
  • Appraisal with comp rents

How to approach a bank

Opening: We're financing a [SF] industrial building in [city] leased to [tenant type] with [term] remaining.

Questions to ask:

  • Do you lend on spec industrial or only stabilized?
  • Typical amortization for industrial?
  • How do you treat rollover in year 1–2?
