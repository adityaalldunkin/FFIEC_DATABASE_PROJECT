Investor office building loan in Texas

Financing for office properties leased to business tenants.

Who this is for

Investors acquiring or refinancing multi-tenant or single-tenant office with lease income as primary repayment source.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks stress tenant credit, lease rollover, and market vacancy. Single-tenant deals hinge on remaining lease term and tenant financials.

Parent category: Investor CRE (income property) · FFIEC: RCONF161, RCONF162 — income-producing commercial real estate

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Rent roll and lease abstracts
  • Tenant financials or public credit ratings
  • Operating statements
  • Environmental Phase I
  • Market vacancy/submarket data

How to approach a bank

Opening: We're financing a [SF] office asset in [city] with [tenant count] tenants and [WALT] years weighted average lease term.

Questions to ask:

  • How do you underwrite near-term lease expirations?
  • Minimum debt yield or DSCR for office in this market?
  • Do you lend on single-tenant credit?
