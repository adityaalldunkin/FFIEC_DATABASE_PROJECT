Investor retail / strip center loan in Texas

Financing for retail pads, strip centers, and shopping centers leased to retailers.

Who this is for

Investors in neighborhood retail, strip centers, or power centers where rent from merchants repays debt.

How community banks underwrite it

Underwriting focuses on tenant sales (where available), co-tenancy clauses, and e-commerce exposure. Anchors and inline shop mix matter.

Parent category: Investor CRE (income property) · FFIEC: RCONF161, RCONF162 — income-producing commercial real estate

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Rent roll with cotenancy summaries
  • Tenant sales reports if available
  • CAM reconciliation
  • Site plan and tenant mix chart
  • Competition/market study

How to approach a bank

Opening: We're acquiring a [count]-tenant retail center in [city]; [anchor] represents [%] of NOI.

Questions to ask:

  • How do you view small-shop vs anchor-heavy centers?
  • Typical LTV for unstabilized retail?
  • Do you finance pad sites on long-term ground leases?
