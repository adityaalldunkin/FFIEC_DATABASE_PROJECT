Multifamily (5+ units) in Texas

Acquisition, refinance, and bridge financing for apartment buildings of five or more units.

Understanding this loan type

Multifamily loans finance apartment properties with five or more units. Texas community banks report balances on Schedule RC-C line RCON1460. Banks with a high multifamily share actively lend on value-add, bridge, and permanent apartment deals.

FFIEC line items: RCON1460 — real estate loans secured by multifamily (5+) residential properties

FAQ

What is a multifamily loan?

Financing for apartment buildings with 5+ units, repaid from rental income.

How do I find a multifamily lender in Texas?

Use portfolio share — banks with 10%+ multifamily concentration specialize in this asset class.

Do community banks do apartment bridge loans?

Many Texas community banks in the $500M–$2B range actively lend on multifamily; check each bank's portfolio mix.
