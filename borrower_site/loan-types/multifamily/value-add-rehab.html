Multifamily value-add / rehab in Texas

Financing for renovations, unit upgrades, and repositioning of apartment communities.

Who this is for

Operators improving rents, occupancy, or amenities at an existing 5+ unit property with a documented capex plan.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks separate hard and soft costs, review contractor bids, and may hold reserves for draws. Underwriting blends current and projected rents with sensitivity on lease-up pace.

Parent category: Multifamily (5+ units) · FFIEC: RCON1460 — real estate loans secured by multifamily (5+) residential properties

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Detailed capex budget and timeline
  • Before/after rent comps
  • Contractor bids or GMP contract
  • Current and pro forma rent roll
  • Draw schedule

How to approach a bank

Opening: We're implementing a $[amount] renovation program at a [unit count]-unit property in [city] to achieve [rent/occupancy target].

Questions to ask:

  • Do you finance capex with the acquisition or as a separate facility?
  • How are construction draws inspected and funded?
  • What LTV on as-stabilized value?

Is this the same as bridge?
