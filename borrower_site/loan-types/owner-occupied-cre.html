Owner-Occupied CRE in Texas

Financing for the building your own business operates from.

Understanding this loan type

Owner-occupied CRE is underwritten on operating business cash flow. Reported as RCONF160. Community banks often pair these with SBA 504 for lower down payments.

FFIEC line items: RCONF160 — loans secured by owner-occupied nonfarm nonresidential properties

FAQ

What is owner-occupied CRE?

A loan to buy or refinance the building where your business operates (51%+ occupancy by your business).

SBA 504 for owner-occupied?

Many Texas community banks participate in SBA 504 alongside conventional owner-occupied CRE.
