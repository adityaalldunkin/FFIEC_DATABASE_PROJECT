Owner-occupied refinance in Texas

Refinance debt on a building your business already owns and occupies.

Who this is for

Established businesses with equity in their facility seeking better terms or cash-out for operations.

How community banks underwrite it

Underwriting uses business cash flow and updated appraisal. Cash-out may fund equipment or working capital with clear use of proceeds.

Parent category: Owner-Occupied CRE · FFIEC: RCONF160 — loans secured by owner-occupied nonfarm nonresidential properties

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Business financials
  • Current loan statement
  • Appraisal or BPO
  • Explanation of cash-out use
  • Entity organizational documents

How to approach a bank

Opening: We've operated from this [city] facility for [years] and want to refinance [$] at improved terms.

Questions to ask:

  • Rate/fixed-term options?
  • Cash-out maximum LTV?
  • Prepayment penalties on existing vs new loan?
