1–4 Family Residential in Texas

Single-family and small residential rental portfolios on bank balance sheets.

Understanding this loan type

1–4 family residential loans (RCON1403) differ from multifamily. Some Texas community banks are residential-heavy; others focus on commercial.

FFIEC line items: RCON1403 — loans secured by 1–4 family residential properties
