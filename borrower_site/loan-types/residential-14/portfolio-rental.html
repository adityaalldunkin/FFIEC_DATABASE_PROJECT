Residential rental portfolio loan in Texas

Finance a basket of 1–4 unit rentals held for income.

Who this is for

Investors building or refinancing portfolios of small residential rentals.

How community banks underwrite it

Banks review aggregate NOI, geographic dispersion, and property management. Blanket loans may cross-collateralize properties.

Parent category: 1–4 Family Residential · FFIEC: RCON1403 — loans secured by 1–4 family residential properties

What to prepare before you call a bank

  • Rent roll across properties
  • Property list with values and debt
  • Operating statements
  • Management agreement if third-party
  • Insurance schedule

How to approach a bank

Opening: We hold [count] rentals in [markets] generating [$] NOI and seek [refinance/acquisition] financing.

Questions to ask:

  • Blanket vs individual property loans?
  • Minimum property count?
  • LTV on portfolio basis?
