What each band means for borrowers

  • <$100M — $250M: Very local; best for small CRE/C&I under $1M where you know the market president.
  • $250M–$500M: Growing commercial capacity; good for $1M–$5M relationship deals.
  • $500M–$2B (ICP): Sweet spot for $2M–$25M credits — 105 banks. ICP directory →
  • $2B–$5B: Regional players; larger tickets, more committee layers.
  • >$5B: 16 megabanks; price-competitive but less relationship-driven.
