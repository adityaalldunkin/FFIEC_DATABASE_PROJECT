Why Texas community banks matter for borrowers

Texas is a commercial-lending state. The typical bank holds 62.0% of assets in loans — mostly CRE and C&I, not credit cards. Unlike generic bank finders, Lenni ranks lenders by actual portfolio mix from regulatory filings, not marketing pages.
