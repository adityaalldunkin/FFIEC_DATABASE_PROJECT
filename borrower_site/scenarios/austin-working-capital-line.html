Austin SaaS company secures a $2M revolving line for growth

Growing technology business · Austin · $2M revolver

An Austin-based B2B software company with $12M revenue needs a working capital line to fund payroll between enterprise contract payments. They have receivables, minimal hard collateral, and a 3-year growth plan.

The situation

C&I is among the most fragmented Texas lending markets — nearly every commercial bank reports some business lending. The challenge is finding a bank whose C&I book is large enough to care about a $2M line but small enough to offer relationship pricing. Austin has growing bank HQ presence; many banks lend statewide from Dallas or San Antonio HQs.

What the data says

  • C&I median at active lenders ~$27M — a $2M line is relevant to community banks
  • Portfolio-style community banks (CRE+C&I >50%) are best fit for relationship C&I
  • CRE specialists often overlap C&I — one bank may do both property and operating line

Related insights: I-01 I-03 I-05 I-11

Action plan

  1. Target ICP banks with C&I share ≥8% and low consumer concentration
  2. Prepare 2 years financials, AR aging, customer concentration, and use-of-funds narrative
  3. Ask about borrowing base vs cash-flow underwriting — structure matters for SaaS
  4. Include 2 Austin-area banks and 3 statewide C&I specialists in your first wave

Questions to ask lenders

  • Do you underwrite on recurring revenue or traditional borrowing base?
  • What covenant package do you use for asset-light businesses?
  • Can this line grow with us without a full re-underwrite?
