Corpus Christi hotel owner refinances a 95-room beachfront property

Hospitality investor · Corpus Christi · $11M refinance

An investor owns a 95-room hotel with ground-floor retail on Corpus Christi Bay. Tourism recovery has stabilized RevPAR; they seek a 10-year fixed refinance with moderate cash-out.

The situation

Hotels sit in investor CRE / income property buckets. At $11M, both larger ICP banks and regionals may participate. Compare your deal size to median and P75 product exposure — at this size you are above median for many community banks, which can be good for engagement but may require regional participation.

What the data says

  • Deal size vs median exposure: $11M is large for many community banks — include regionals
  • Perform lender diligence: ask about hospitality exposure and 90+ day past-due trends
  • Hospitality is specialized — prioritize banks that already hold hotel or mixed-use CRE

Related insights: I-03 I-09

Action plan

  1. Prepare 3-year STR report, franchise agreement summary, and capital expenditure plan
  2. Mix community bank outreach with 2–3 regional players for capacity
  3. Ask specifically about hospitality concentration limits
  4. Disclose seasonality and hurricane insurance costs in first call

Questions to ask lenders

  • What is your maximum single-asset hospitality exposure?
  • Do you require franchise approval for refinance?
  • How do you underwrite insurance and wind coverage in coastal markets?
