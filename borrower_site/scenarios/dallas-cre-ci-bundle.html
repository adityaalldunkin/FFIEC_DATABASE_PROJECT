Dallas sponsor bundles warehouse debt and operating line

Diversified commercial sponsor · Dallas · $6M CRE + $1.5M line

A Dallas sponsor owns two light-industrial properties and an operating company that distributes building materials. They want to refinance both properties and upsize their working capital line in one banking relationship.

The situation

EDA co-occurrence analysis shows investor CRE specialists frequently overlap with C&I specialists. Banks with both ≥8% investor CRE and ≥8% C&I are ideal for bundled requests. One relationship can reduce legal cost and covenant complexity vs splitting across banks.

What the data says

  • CRE + C&I cross-sell: many full-relationship banks bundle property and operating credit
  • Dallas has the deepest specialist bench — verify branch coverage, not just HQ
  • 105 ICP banks statewide — relationship bundling works best in $500M–$2B segment

Related insights: I-11 I-04 I-06

Action plan

  1. Filter banks where both investor CRE and C&I exceed 8% portfolio share
  2. Prepare consolidated sponsor financials plus property-level rent rolls
  3. Pitch total relationship: deposits, treasury, future acquisitions
  4. Negotiate cross-collateralization terms carefully — ask what's required vs optional

Questions to ask lenders

  • Can CRE and C&I be under one credit agreement or separate facilities?
  • Do you offer relationship pricing for combined exposure?
  • What reporting package do you require for consolidated sponsors?
