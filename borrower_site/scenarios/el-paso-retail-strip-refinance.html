El Paso investor refinances a neighborhood retail strip

Retail CRE investor · El Paso · $2.4M refinance

A local investor owns a 12-unit neighborhood strip center with mixed local tenants and one anchor grocer. Occupancy is 92%; they want to refinance out of a maturing loan and fund facade improvements.

The situation

Retail CRE competes in the broad investor CRE pool. El Paso has fewer HQ banks than Dallas or Houston, but many Texas community banks lend statewide from branch networks. Use branch city data, not just HQ city, when building your list.

What the data says

  • HQ city ≠ lending territory — FDIC branch data shows where banks actually operate
  • Investor CRE market is fragmented — build an 8–10 bank competitive process
  • Median CRE proxy at Texas banks ~$116M — $2.4M is material for community lenders

Related insights: I-07 I-02

Action plan

  1. Include El Paso branch banks plus statewide retail-leaning specialists
  2. Prepare tenant roster, lease expirations, and CAM reconciliation
  3. Highlight grocer anchor credit and local foot-traffic story
  4. Compare community bank vs regional pricing — both may participate at this size

Questions to ask lenders

  • What tenant concentration limits do you apply on retail strips?
  • Will you finance TI reserves in the refinance?
  • Do you lend in El Paso from a non-local HQ?
