Houston logistics owner refinances a 120,000 SF warehouse

Industrial property investor · Houston · $8.5M refinance

A Houston investor owns a single-tenant logistics warehouse on the North Belt with 7 years remaining on a NNN lease. They want to pull equity for their next acquisition and extend the loan term.

The situation

Investor CRE (income property) has one of the widest lender pools in Texas — most banks report non-zero balances. Houston is the second-largest banking HQ market. For an $8.5M refi, both ICP community banks ($500M–$2B) and larger regionals can participate; the question is who already likes industrial exposure.

What the data says

  • Investor CRE has high availability — competitive process with 10–15 banks is realistic
  • Median active-lender investor CRE exposure ~$116M statewide — an $8.5M deal is meaningful
  • Consumer lending is the most concentrated product; CRE is fragmented — shop broadly

Related insights: I-01 I-02 I-03 I-06

Action plan

  1. Prepare trailing-12 NOI, lease abstract, and tenant credit summary
  2. Shortlist Houston HQ banks plus statewide industrial-leaning specialists from portfolio mix
  3. Disclose desired cash-out amount early — some community banks cap proceeds
  4. Run parallel conversations with 3 ICP banks and 2 regionals for pricing leverage

Questions to ask lenders

  • What DSCR and LTV do you require on single-tenant industrial?
  • Do you offer interest-only periods on refinance?
  • What is your maximum hold per property type?
