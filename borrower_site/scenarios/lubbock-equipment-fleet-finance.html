Lubbock contractor finances $1.2M equipment fleet

Commercial contractor · Lubbock · $1.2M term + $500K line

A West Texas commercial contractor wins larger municipal contracts and needs to finance excavators, trucks, and trailers plus a line for payroll between draws.

The situation

Equipment finance appears in C&I and lease financing lines. West Texas community banks often understand contractor cycles. For equipment + working capital, C&I specialists with low consumer mix are the right target — not mortgage-heavy banks.

What the data says

  • C&I has the widest lender availability — competitive terms possible with 8–10 banks
  • Portfolio-style community banks (not consumer-heavy) fit contractor relationships
  • Lease financing (RCON1754) is a separate narrow pool — most deals sit in C&I term debt

Related insights: I-01 I-05 I-10

Action plan

  1. Prepare equipment quotes, contract backlog, and bonding capacity letter
  2. Target Lubbock and West Texas C&I banks with contractor experience
  3. Ask about UCC filings vs titled equipment loans — structure affects rate
  4. Bundle operating line in same credit request for efficiency

Questions to ask lenders

  • Do you finance new and used heavy equipment equally?
  • What advance rates apply to fleet collateral?
  • Can the line resize with contract awards?
