Midland developer builds a spec industrial pad in Permian Basin

Commercial developer · Midland · $5.5M construction loan

A Midland developer breaks ground on a 60,000 SF spec industrial building with pre-leasing interest from oilfield services tenants. They need a construction loan with mini-perm option.

The situation

Construction lending is reported on RCONF158/RCONF159 — a smaller specialist pool than stabilized CRE. Permian Basin cycles matter: banks active in construction here often have experience with energy-correlated tenant demand. Check 5-quarter trend — growing construction books signal appetite.

What the data says

  • Construction is a narrower pool — expect 6–8 targeted calls, not 15-bank spray
  • Review lender 90+ day past-due ratios — elevated stress warrants extra diligence questions
  • Market momentum: ask if their construction book grew over the last 5 quarters

Related insights: I-08 I-12 I-09

Action plan

  1. Target banks with construction ≥8% and Midland/Odessa branch presence
  2. Prepare full construction budget, GC contract, and pre-leasing LOIs
  3. Stress tenant demand under lower oil price scenario in your narrative
  4. Negotiate extension options and takeout criteria upfront

Questions to ask lenders

  • What pre-leasing percentage do you require before funding?
  • Do you offer construction-to-mini-perm in one approval?
  • How do you handle cost overruns and draw inspections?
