San Antonio entrepreneur buys a multi-unit restaurant group

Food & beverage entrepreneur · San Antonio · $1.8M acquisition + $300K working capital

An experienced restaurant operator acquires three casual-dining locations from a retiring owner. They need acquisition financing plus working capital for a rebrand and staffing ramp.

The situation

Business acquisition loans sit in C&I buckets. San Antonio has major regional banks (Frost, Broadway National) plus many community banks. For sub-$2M deals, ICP community banks often move faster than regionals if the sponsor has relevant operating history.

What the data says

  • Median ICP bank loans ~$700M gross — a $1.8M acquisition is relationship-sized
  • Disclose deal size early — below-median exposures may not clear committee thresholds at some banks
  • Ask lenders if their restaurant/Hospitality book grew in the last 5 quarters

Related insights: I-03 I-06 I-08

Action plan

  1. Prepare quality-of-earnings summary, store-level P&Ls, and transition plan
  2. Target C&I specialists with low consumer concentration — avoid credit-card-heavy banks
  3. Pitch relationship value: deposits, merchant services, future real estate needs
  4. Contact 8–10 banks; acquisition finance is competitive in Texas C&I market

Questions to ask lenders

  • Have you financed restaurant acquisitions in the last 24 months?
  • What seller note or equity injection do you require?
  • Is working capital included in one facility or a separate line?
