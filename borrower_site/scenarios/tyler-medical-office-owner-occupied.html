Tyler physician group buys its clinic building

Medical practice partners · Tyler · $4.6M acquisition

A 6-physician orthopedic group wants to purchase their 28,000 SF clinic from a REIT landlord. Stable patient volume and strong practice financials support the acquisition.

The situation

Medical office can be owner-occupied or investor CRE depending on structure. For a user-occupant practice, owner-occupied CRE underwriting ties to practice cash flow. Tyler has community banks (Southside Bank HQ nearby) and East Texas lenders with healthcare exposure.

What the data says

  • Filter out consumer-heavy banks — prioritize portfolio-style commercial lenders
  • Community banks prefer user-occupied deals with strong operating entity financials
  • Median Texas bank assets ~$433M — physician groups are prized deposit relationships

Related insights: I-05 I-10

Action plan

  1. Present practice financials, physician comp structure, and payer mix
  2. Target East Texas community banks with owner-occupied CRE share ≥8%
  3. Discuss deposit balances and treasury services — strengthens community bank proposals
  4. Get appraisal and Phase I early — medical sites may trigger environmental review

Questions to ask lenders

  • Do you have healthcare practice lending experience?
  • What DSCR do you require on medical owner-occupied?
  • Can tenant improvements for imaging equipment be financed?
