Waco manufacturer buys its headquarters building

Owner-operator manufacturer · Waco · $3.1M purchase + improvements

A precision machining company renting in Waco finds its 45,000 SF building for sale. They want to own the facility, consolidate operations, and stop paying rent to a landlord who may sell to a competitor.

The situation

Waco has several strong ICP banks including some of the largest loan portfolios in the $500M–$2B band statewide. Owner-occupied CRE is reported on RCONF160 — a distinct line from investor CRE. Community banks in Central Texas often prefer user-occupied deals because cash flow ties directly to the operating business.

What the data says

  • FIRST NATIONAL BANK OF CENTRAL TEXAS (Waco) ranks among top ICP loan books statewide
  • Owner-occupied CRE is a separate Call Report bucket from investor income property
  • Community banks (FFIEC 041) median portfolio ~$250M — $3M deals are committee material

Related insights: I-05 I-06 I-07

Action plan

  1. Start with Waco HQ banks and Central Texas branches of statewide community banks
  2. Combine business financials with property NOI — underwriters blend operating company and real estate
  3. Get environmental and property condition reports before term sheet — speeds community bank credit
  4. Ask about SBA 504 pairing if equity injection is tight

Questions to ask lenders

  • What LTV do you offer on owner-occupied manufacturing?
  • Do you require the business to occupy 51%+ of the building?
  • Can equipment financing be bundled in the same relationship?
